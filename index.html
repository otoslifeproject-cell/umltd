Where it fits

Designed for real operational constraints.

Connected systems do not always live in offices or datacentres. They may be installed in temporary, distributed or low-infrastructure environments where the communications path needs to be simple, resilient and manageable.

Remote locations

Mobile-network communications for equipment operating where fixed internet access is unavailable, impractical or unnecessary.

Unattended equipment

Device communications that can support periodic reporting, status messages, alerts and exception-driven monitoring.

Distributed estates

A consistent communications approach across multiple sites, nodes, devices or field installations.

Customer communications

SMS broadcasts, service notifications and campaign messaging when organisations need a direct, established mobile channel.
