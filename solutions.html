Solutions

Connectivity and communications built around the job.

From dedicated long numbers and cellular IoT to remote telemetry, SMS broadcasting and customer communications, our services are designed to support systems that need dependable information flow.

01Dedicated long numbers

We provide managed rental and administration of dedicated long numbers for organisations that require an identifiable mobile communications endpoint for devices, applications or messaging workflows.

Rather than treating the number as a one-off component, the service can be managed as part of the wider connectivity requirement, giving organisations a clearer operational route for provisioning, replacement and ongoing support.

  • Dedicated number provision
  • Managed rental and administration
  • Messaging and device use cases
  • Integration with operational workflows

02IoT & M2M connectivity

Cellular connectivity is well suited to devices that operate outside the normal office network, particularly where Wi-Fi, fixed broadband or local infrastructure is unavailable or undesirable.

We support machine-to-machine and IoT communications for equipment that needs to send operational data, receive instructions, report status or remain reachable across distributed environments.

  • SIM and cellular connectivity
  • Machine-to-machine communications
  • Remote and low-infrastructure locations
  • Distributed device estates

03Remote telemetry & monitoring

Connected equipment often needs to report more than whether it is online. Telemetry services can carry sensor values, equipment states, events and exception conditions to a central system for monitoring or action.

Typical use cases include environmental measurements, equipment status, level or flow information, alarm conditions and scheduled operational reports. The service remains deliberately application-agnostic so it can support different types of connected assets and monitoring platforms.

  • Sensor and status data
  • Scheduled or event-driven reporting
  • Operational alerts
  • Monitoring platform integration

04SMS broadcasting & notifications

SMS remains a direct and widely supported channel for operational messages and customer communications. We support outbound messaging requirements ranging from service notifications and alerts to planned broadcasts and campaign communications.

The emphasis is on using messaging as part of a defined communications workflow: the right audience, the right message and a clear operational purpose.

  • SMS broadcasts
  • Service and status notifications
  • Operational alerts
  • Customer updates and campaigns

05Marketing communications

Universal Marketing Ltd also supports practical marketing communications, particularly where messaging and customer engagement sit alongside the wider communications infrastructure.

This can include outbound SMS activity, customer notifications, campaign support and straightforward communications planning. The objective is not unnecessary complexity; it is to help organisations use the channels they already have more effectively.

  • Customer messaging
  • SMS campaign support
  • Notifications and engagement
  • Communications planning

06Integration & support

Connectivity is most useful when it becomes part of the operational system around it. We can support the connection of device data, alerts and messaging events into monitoring platforms, applications and internal workflows.

Requirements vary by deployment, so the integration approach is kept pragmatic: establish what needs to move, where it needs to go and how the organisation needs to work with it.

  • Connected-system integration
  • Messaging workflow support
  • Telemetry data hand-off
  • Ongoing service support

Need a combination of these services?

Many requirements cross more than one category — for example cellular connectivity, a dedicated number, telemetry and alerting. We can scope the communications layer as a joined-up service.
