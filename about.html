About us

Practical communications for connected operations.

Universal Marketing Ltd is a UK-registered company providing managed connectivity, messaging, telemetry and communications services for organisations with distributed systems and customer communications requirements.

Our approach

Technology should solve the communications problem, not become another one.

Connected services are often most valuable in places where conventional infrastructure is least convenient: remote locations, unattended equipment, distributed sites and systems that need to report status without depending on a local user or fixed network connection.

Our role is to help make that communications layer manageable. That may mean provisioning dedicated long numbers, providing cellular connectivity, carrying telemetry, enabling SMS alerts or supporting customer-facing messaging.

We work from the operational requirement outward. The aim is a clear service that fits the application, integrates with the systems around it and can be supported as requirements change.

Application-agnostic

We support the communications layer across different devices, platforms and operational use cases rather than tying the service to a single product.

Operationally focused

We consider what has to happen when a device reports, an alert is raised or a message needs to reach a customer.

Designed to adapt

Connected estates and communications requirements evolve, so services should be capable of changing with them.

How we operate

A small company should still operate like a serious supplier.

We keep the operating model deliberately straightforward: understand the requirement, document responsibilities, work within provider constraints and maintain enough service information to support the customer properly once a deployment is live.

Requirement-led

We start with the operational problem and communications flow rather than forcing every requirement into a fixed product template.

Supplier-aware

Where underlying network or platform providers set specific conditions, those dependencies form part of the service design.

Documented

Key customer, service and approval information is recorded so the basis of a deployment is understandable later.

Supportable

Services should be designed so that changes, faults and operational questions can be handled without reconstructing the original project from scratch.
